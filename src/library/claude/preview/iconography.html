Rules

Updated 2026-05-10 per 05_Update-Rules.
Solid, single-colour, currentColorLock to filled glyphs. Tint via CSS, never hard-code a fill.
No outline / hairline iconsThey read as broken on the deep-black canvas.
Eva for nav arrows, Solar for objectsBoth are filled and visually compatible; mix freely.
No emoji in product chromeStatus uses coloured dots, never emoji.
16 / 20 / 24 onlyMatch the Figma component sizes. Nothing in between.
Don't combine icons in a single glyphOne concept, one icon. Use a chip for compound state.
