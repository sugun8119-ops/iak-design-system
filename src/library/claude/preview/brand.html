Rules · summary

Updated 2026-05-10 per 05_Update-Rules. Logo only — chrome motif rules live with the product hero documentation.
Do
  • Use in sidebar, top-bar, navigation and identity surfaces.
  • Maintain original proportions and original colour.
  • Reserve clear space ≥ ½ × mark height on every side.
  • Render as a plain raster on the canvas — no decoration.
Don't
  • Stretch or scale non-uniformly.
  • Crop the gem or use a partial slice.
  • Apply outer glow, halo or drop-shadow.
  • Place on a blue or coloured glow background.
  • Use as a hero or oversized promotional image.
  • Pair with the chrome graphic motif in the same composition.
