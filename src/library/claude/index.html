The five non-negotiables

Do / Don't

Pinned at the top of every screen review. Patches before redesigns.

Do
  • Reach for semantic tokens — never raw hex in product code.
  • Use Pretendard for Korean, English, and numerals.
  • Express focus as 1 px accent border + inset 1 px ring.
  • Pair every status colour with text and an icon.
  • Use a single primary CTA per surface.
Don't
  • Introduce a second brand accent.
  • Bold body copy for emphasis — use a chip / label.
  • Use outer glow / halo to indicate focus.
  • Place the gem mark on coloured glow or hero surfaces.
  • Mix icon families within a screen.
