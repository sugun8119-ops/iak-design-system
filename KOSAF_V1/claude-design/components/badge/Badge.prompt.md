Short status label (`KOSAF/Badge`, Figma 218:621) — use for 2–5 character states such as 답변완료 next to rows or titles.

```jsx
<Badge>답변완료</Badge>
```

- One variant only in source: 88×30 (content-width here), radius 15, #EBFFE9 fill, #059B00 Medium 14, 18px side padding.
- Do not invent red/yellow/blue badge tones; they are not defined in KOSAF_V1.
